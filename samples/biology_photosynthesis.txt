Photosynthesis is a vital biological mechanism utilized by terrestrial flora, cyanobacteria, and aquatic algae to convert ambient photon energy into stable chemical energy stored within polysaccharide carbohydrates. Cellular chloroplasts containing green chlorophyll pigments absorb specific wavelengths of solar radiation, initiating the light-dependent photochemical reactions within thylakoid membrane structures. Water molecules undergo photolysis, releasing molecular oxygen as a byproduct while generating ATP and NADPH for the subsequent Calvin cycle.
