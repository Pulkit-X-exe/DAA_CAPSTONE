Quantum computing represents a paradigm shift in computational theory by exploiting the principles of quantum mechanics, specifically superposition and entanglement. Unlike classical computing systems that represent binary states as discrete zeros and ones, quantum processors manipulate quantum bits or qubits. These physical systems execute specialized quantum algorithms, such as Shor's algorithm for prime integer factorization and Grover's search algorithm for unstructured database querying, demonstrating theoretical exponential speedups over traditional Von Neumann architectures.
